Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Serene: Mindful Soundscapes ("the App") is a mindfulness and relaxation application designed to provide calming soundscapes, meditation experiences, and personalised audio sessions. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

Serene is designed with privacy in mind. The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, contacts, or precise location.

The application may store preferences and settings locally on your device to provide app functionality, such as saved sound combinations and user settings.

You can stop using the application at any time. You can also remove stored application data by uninstalling the App or clearing application storage through your device settings.
